# Saving policy

**Save without asking**: durable facts that will matter in future conversations:
- who I am, how I work, and my preferences
- corrections or confirmed approaches I give you (as `feedback`, with Why / How to apply)
- ongoing projects, goals, deadlines (absolute dates) and constraints
- where my information lives (tools, links, folders)

**Never save**:
- passwords, API keys, tokens, account or card numbers, government IDs
- details that only matter to the current conversation
- what my files, repos or docs already record; point to them instead
- private details about other people beyond what the work needs

**Ask me first, every time**, before saving anything about my personal health, religion or religious practice, finances, sexual orientation, politics, legal matters, or family and relationships, even if I mentioned it. Facts about client organizations I work with are normal work context and don't need asking.

**When unsure**, don't save; mention at the end of your reply what you would remember and let me confirm.
